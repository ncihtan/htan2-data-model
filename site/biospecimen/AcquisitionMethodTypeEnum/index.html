    name: AcquisitionMethodTypeEnum
title: acquisition_method_type
from_schema: https://w3id.org/htan/acquisition_method_type_enum
permissible_values:
  Autopsy:
    text: Autopsy
    description: Autopsy; an examination and dissection of a dead body to determine
      cause of death or the changes produced by disease.
  Biopsy:
    text: Biopsy
    description: The removal of tissue specimens or fluid from the living body for
      microscopic examination, performed to establish a diagnosis.
  Blood draw:
    text: Blood draw
    description: A small volume of blood removed for testing or storage.
  Core needle biopsy:
    text: Core needle biopsy
    description: The removal of a tissue sample using a needle with a relatively large
      diameter, for microscopic examination.
  Excisional Biopsy:
    text: Excisional Biopsy
    description: A surgical procedure in which an entire lesion is removed for microscopic
      examination.
  Fine needle aspirate:
    text: Fine needle aspirate
    description: The removal of tissue or fluid with a thin needle for examination
      under a microscope.
  Forceps Biopsy:
    text: Forceps Biopsy
    description: The removal of a tissue sample using a specially-designed endoscope
      consisting of a slender flexible forceps with movable cup-shaped jaws. The jaws
      are closed around the tissue to be collected and while closed the forceps are
      withdrawn to capture the biopsy tissue.
  Incisional Biopsy:
    text: Incisional Biopsy
    description: A surgical procedure in which part of a lesion is removed for microscopic
      examination.
  Lymphadenectomy (regional nodes):
    text: Lymphadenectomy (regional nodes)
    description: 'Surgical removal of lymph nodes, usually done to assess the spread
      of cancer: A lymph node that drains lymph from a region of interest.'
  Not applicable:
    text: Not applicable
    description: Determination of a value is not relevant in the current context.
  Not reported:
    text: Not reported
    description: Not provided or available.
  Not specified:
    text: Not specified
    description: Not stated explicitly or in detail.
  Other:
    text: Other
    description: Different than the one(s) previously specified or mentioned.
  Pancreaticoduodenectomy:
    text: Pancreaticoduodenectomy
    description: Surgical excision of all or part of the pancreas and duodenum.
  Punch biopsy:
    text: Punch biopsy
    description: Removal of a disk of skin tissue, for microscopic examination, using
      a punch.
  Re-excision:
    text: Re-excision
    description: A surgical procedure to remove the remaining abnormal tissues from
      the body, not completely removed in an earlier attempt.
  Sentinel node biopsy:
    text: Sentinel node biopsy
    description: Surgical removal and examination of the sentinel node, the lymph
      node which is first in receiving the lymphatic drainage of a specified anatomic
      location and is most likely to be affected by the lymphatic spread of cancer.
  Shave biopsy:
    text: Shave biopsy
    description: Removal skin tissue, for microscopic examination, using a blade oriented
      in the horizontal plane of the skin surface.
  Surgical Resection:
    text: Surgical Resection
    description: The surgical removal of a lesion, often as part of a biopsy and with
      healthy margins.
  Unknown:
    text: Unknown
    description: Not known, not observed, not recorded, or refused.
