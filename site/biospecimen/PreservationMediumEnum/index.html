permissible_values:
  Desiccant:
    text: Desiccant
    description: A material that absorbs moisture from its surrounding environment.
  EDTA:
    text: EDTA
    description: The acid form of edetate, a chelating agent with anti-hypercalcemic
      and anticoagulant properties. Edetic acid binds calcium and heavy metal ions,
      forming soluble stable complexes which are readily excreted by the kidneys.
      This results in a decrease in serum calcium levels. This agent is also used
      as an anticoagulant for blood specimens and is applied as a treatment of lead
      poisoning.
  FFPE:
    text: FFPE
    description: Refers to samples that have been preserved with formalin and then
      embedded into a paraffin block for sectioning.
  Formalin Fixed - Buffered:
    text: Formalin Fixed - Buffered
    description: The use of buffered formalin for preservation of tissue samples.
  Formalin Fixed - Unbuffered:
    text: Formalin Fixed - Unbuffered
    description: The use of unbuffered formalin for preservation of tissue samples.
  Formalin Fixed Tissue:
    text: Formalin Fixed Tissue
    description: A tissue sample that has been fixed with formalin.
  Glutaraldehyde:
    text: Glutaraldehyde
    description: A water-soluble oily liquid, C5H8O2, containing two aldehyde groups,
      used as a disinfectant and as a fixative for biological tissues.
  Isopentane:
    text: Isopentane
    description: An organic, branched-chain alkane with five carbon atoms.
  Liquid Nitrogen:
    text: Liquid Nitrogen
    description: Nitrogen in a liquid state, having been super-cooled to about -200
      degrees Celsius.
  Liquid Nitrogen Vapor:
    text: Liquid Nitrogen Vapor
    description: The gaseous state of liquid nitrogen with a temperature of -167 degrees
      Celsius.
  Not Applicable:
    text: Not Applicable
    description: Determination of a value is not relevant in the current context.
  Not Reported:
    text: Not Reported
    description: Not provided or available.
  OCT:
    text: OCT
    description: Optimal Cutting Temperature Compound - A solution of water soluble
      glycols and resins that provide a specimen support matrix for cryostat sectioning
      at temperatures of -10 degrees C and below.
  Paraffin Block:
    text: Paraffin Block
    description: Tissue that is preserved and embedded in paraffin.
  RNALater:
    text: RNALater
    description: Trade name for an aqueous, non-toxic tissue storage reagent that
      rapidly permeates tissues to stabilize and protect cellular RNA.
  TRIzol:
    text: TRIzol
    description: A ready-to-use reagent for the isolation of high-quality total RNA
      or the simultaneous isolation of RNA, DNA, and protein from biological samples.
      It is a monophasic solution of phenol and guanidine isothiocyanate.
  Unknown:
    text: Unknown
    description: Not known, observed, recorded; or reported as unknown by the data
